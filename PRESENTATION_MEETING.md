## 🌟 SECTION 2: PROJECT OVERVIEW & MISSION

### **The Challenge:**
- Limited access to physical science labs in Tanzanian schools
- Gender gaps in STEM participation
- Need for accessible, inclusive learning experiences
- Barriers to hands-on experimentation and exploration

### **Our Solution: Adilisha STEM Lab**
A comprehensive digital learning platform that delivers:
- **Browser-based and VR experiences** - no expensive hardware required
- **Immersive STEM labs** - accessible everywhere, anytime
- **Curriculum-aligned content** - tailored for Tanzanian education system
- **Multi-level learning pathways** - from primary to advanced levels

### **Target Audience:**
- **Primary Explorer** (Standards 1-7): Ages 6-13
- **Secondary Innovator** (Forms 1-4): Ages 14-17
- **Advanced Creator** (A-Level & Youth Clubs): Ages 18+

### **Core Subject Areas:**
1. **Biology & Human Health** - Ecosystems, anatomy, wellness simulations
2. **Chemistry Reimagined** - Virtual experiments, reaction visualizations
3. **Physics & Engineering** - Forces, flight, energy concepts
4. **Mathematics Labs** - Visualized algebra, geometry, data science

### **Q3: What is your technology stack, and why did you choose it?**
**Answer:**
"We're using Laravel 12 (PHP 8.2) as our backend framework, with modern frontend technologies including Tailwind CSS. We chose Laravel because:
- Strong ecosystem for web applications
- Good performance and scalability
- Active community and support
- Cost-effective hosting options
- Our team's expertise with PHP

For 3D models, we're using Google Model Viewer for browser-based 3D visualization. We're open to exploring more advanced simulation technologies and are considering options like Unity WebGL, Three.js, or specialized educational simulation platforms."
